How to fly

Turn on Fly camera, then: drag to look around the point in front of you, right-drag (or Shift+drag) to slide, wheel to move in and out. W A S D move, Q E go down and up (hold Shift for faster). Add key here stores the view at the current time. To change a key: click Select next to it (the view jumps there), move the camera, click Replace selected key; it keeps its time. Moving the time slider deselects. Copy here puts that key's view at the current time. Two keys = one smooth move; one key = a still camera. Save cameras writes cameras.json; the final render uses it.
