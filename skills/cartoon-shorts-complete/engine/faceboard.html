Face and expression board

Every face is drawn by src/face2d.js from emotion values (0 to 1), not from presets. Each card shows the emotion mix that makes it. Mix them freely: the face reads in between.
  • Both eyes are always the same type (dots, rings, lids, chevrons or arcs). An eye type never fades: it switches, hidden inside a blink.
  • Brows lead, the mouth comes last: the brows move first, the eyes switch in a blink, and the mouth snaps into place after.
  • The head gives a small jolt or bounce on every change, then holds still. Blinks every 2 to 5 s while calm.
  • Faces stay white in every light (un-tone-mapped card). A back view shows no face.
  • Talking: life.talk opens the mouth on the voice. Uneven brows (doubt) give the most life to a calm face.
